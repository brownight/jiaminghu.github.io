A personal philosophy

Revolver Theory左轮理论

Be firm about what matters.
Stay flexible about how you get there.

I want to become deeply knowledgeable and do work that has an impact. These ambitions give me direction. But I do not need to decide in advance exactly when, where, or through which path they must happen.

I think of this as a revolver. The ambition is to fire the shot; the particular opportunities are the chambers. Each serious attempt is a pull of the trigger. Staying open to a different path is giving the cylinder another turn.

If I fix both the destination and every detail of the route, I may keep trying the same empty chamber. Allowing some randomness leaves room for an opportunity I could not have planned.

I want to give each attempt my best, while remaining willing to change how I try.

Firm in my flexibility.
Flexible in my conviction.

我希望成为一个领域的专家，做有影响力的事。这些大方向给了我目标，但我不必把自己限定在某个时间、某个领域，或某一条具体的路上。

我把它比作一把左轮：人生目标是把子弹射出去，具体的机会是弹巢里的不同位置。每一次全力以赴，都是扣动扳机；给具体的路径留一点随机性，就是转一转弹巢。

如果既锁死目标，又锁死实现它的每一个细节，就可能一直对着同一个空弹巢扣动扳机。适当地允许随机性，是给机遇留空间，也是给自己留下成功的契机。

每次尝试都认真投入，也始终愿意调整尝试的方式。

坚定地灵活，
灵活地坚定。
