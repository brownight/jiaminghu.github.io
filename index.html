Jiaming Hu

About

I am a PhD student in Statistics at Boston University, advised by Debarghya Mukherjee and Ioannis Paschalidis. I received my undergraduate degree in Mathematics with Honors from New York University.

Previously, I worked at Moderna on automating scientific workflows with multi-agent systems. I now work at Unity, developing recursive self-improvement (RSI) methods informed by human–agent interactions.

Research interests

  • Game-theoretic approaches to AI. I study alignment and robustness in deep learning models through game theory and minimax optimization.
  • AI evaluation. I contribute to multiple AI evaluation projects that explore the capabilities and limitations of AI systems. I am curious to see what the arrival of weak-to-strong supervision will bring.
  • Following my curiosity. I enjoy exploring new questions and directions beyond these themes, including JEPA world models, personalization, and tabular foundation models.
